Dear You: Letters for Hard and Ordinary Days

Short, original letters read aloud, for the mornings you don't want to start, the evenings that feel too quiet, and the conversations you've been avoiding. Every letter ends with one small, doable step and one question worth writing back to. A minute or two each.

  1. Letter 1: For the morning you don't want to startAn original two-minute letter for a fresh start. I know this morning feels heavier than it should. You woke up already tired, already behind, already listing what went wrong yesterday.
  2. Letter 2: When you feel behind everyone elseAn original two-minute letter for a little encouragement. You've been measuring again. Someone your age with the house, the title, the calm life. Someone younger, further along.
  3. Letter 3: Before the hard conversationAn original two-minute letter for courage for something hard. There's a conversation you've been circling. You've rehearsed it in the shower, in the car, at two in the morning.
  4. Letter 4: When your mind won't slow downAn original two-minute letter for a moment of calm. Your thoughts are all talking at once right now. That's alright. Nothing needs to be solved in the next minute.
  5. Letter 5: A thank-you you haven't saidAn original two-minute letter for a moment of gratitude. Somewhere back there is a person who helped you and never heard about it. A teacher. A neighbor. Someone who said one kind thing at exactly the right time.
  6. Letter 6: On the day you made a mistakeAn original two-minute letter for a little encouragement. You got something wrong. Maybe it was small and you can't stop replaying it. Maybe it was big and you're bracing for what comes next.
  7. Letter 7: When you feel invisibleAn original two-minute letter for a little encouragement. Some days it feels like you could disappear and nobody would notice until the bills came. That feeling is real, and it's heavy.
  8. Letter 8: Starting over, againAn original two-minute letter for a fresh start. You're starting again. Maybe for the third time. Maybe the tenth. And part of you is whispering, why bother, you know how this ends.
  9. Letter 9: For a lonely eveningAn original two-minute letter for a moment of calm. The evening is quiet, and the quiet feels a little too loud tonight.
  10. Letter 10: When you're too hard on yourselfAn original two-minute letter for a little encouragement. Listen to the voice in your head for a moment. Would you ever talk to someone you love like that?
  11. Letter 11: Before something newAn original two-minute letter for courage for something hard. Something new is close. A job, a move, a first day, a first conversation. And the nerves are loud.
  12. Letter 12: The small good thingsAn original two-minute letter for a moment of gratitude. Today wasn't remarkable. Maybe it was even a little hard. But somewhere in it there were small good things, and they deserve to be noticed before they slip away.
  13. Letter 13: When you can't decideAn original two-minute letter for a moment of calm. You've been going back and forth. Pros, cons, what ifs. The longer you think, the foggier it gets.
  14. Letter 14: A letter for a year from nowAn original two-minute letter for a surprise. Today, let's write to the person you'll be a year from now.
