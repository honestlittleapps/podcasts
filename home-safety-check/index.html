One Room, One Check

Short episodes that walk you through one quick home safety check per room: smoke alarms, trip hazards, cords, kitchens and stairs. General information; for anything electrical, structural or urgent, contact a qualified professional.

  2. Trip hazards in the hallwayWalk your main hallway slowly and look down.
  3. The kitchenLook at the area around your stove.
  4. Cords and outletsCheck for frayed cords or overloaded power strips.
