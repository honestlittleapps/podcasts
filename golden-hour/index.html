Golden Hour Notes

Short episodes about watching and photographing sunsets: golden hour, blue hour, cloud cover, where to stand, and phone camera tips.

Made by Honest Little Apps, the makers of Asterfold: Sunset Planner, an iPhone sunset planner for the places you actually watch from. Narrated with a synthetic (text-to-speech) voice.

  1. What golden hour really isGolden hour is the time shortly before sunset, and shortly after sunrise, when the sun is low in the sky.
  2. Don't leave at sunsetMany people pack up the moment the sun touches the horizon.
  3. Clouds helpA completely clear sky can make a plain sunset.
  4. Phone camera tipsTap on the bright part of the sky, then slide the exposure down a little so the colours aren't washed out.
  5. Pick your spot earlyLook for a spot with a clear view toward the west, without tall buildings in the way.
