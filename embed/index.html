A free "Proverbs of the day" player for your website

Proverbs has 31 chapters, and many people read the one that matches today's date. This little player does that automatically: it plays today's chapter, read aloud from the World English Bible (public domain). It's free, there's no sign-up, and it has no ads.

Add it to your site

Paste this where you want the player to appear (church websites, ministry pages, blogs, newsletters that allow embeds):
