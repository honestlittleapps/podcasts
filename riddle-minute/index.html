Riddle Minute: Number and Logic Riddles

One-minute number and logic riddles for puzzle lovers. Hear the riddle, pause and think, then hear the answer and why it works. Good for car rides, classrooms and family dinners.

  1. The bat and the ballA bat and a ball cost one dollar and ten cents in total. The bat costs one dollar more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?
  2. Three switchesThree light switches are outside a closed room. One controls the bulb inside. You can flip switches as much as you like, but you can open the door only once. How do you find the right switch?
  3. Doubling lily padsA patch of lily pads doubles in size every day. It takes forty-eight days to cover the whole lake. How long does it take to cover half the lake?
  4. Count the handshakesFive people meet, and everyone shakes hands with everyone else exactly once. How many handshakes are there?
  5. Eight eightsUsing only addition, how can you add eight eights to get the number one thousand?
