Sip Cues: Tiny Hydration Habits

One-minute episodes with one small, practical habit for drinking more water: linking a sip to things you already do, using the bottle you already carry, and making it easy to remember. General wellness information, not medical advice; your own fluid needs depend on you, your activity and your doctor's guidance.
